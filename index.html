Scams to watch out for

Most scams follow the same few patterns. If you know them, they're much easier to spot.

📦

The package problem

"Your delivery is on hold. Update your address here."

Red flag: a strange link and a rush to act. Real carriers don't text you random links.

👵

The family emergency

"Grandma, it's me. I'm in trouble. Please don't tell Mom."

Red flag: asking for secrecy and money fast. Hang up and call your grandchild directly.

🏦

The bank alert

"Suspicious activity on your account. Move your money to stay safe."

Red flag: your bank will never ask you to move money or share a code. Call the number on your card.

🏛️

The government call

"This is Social Security. Your number has been suspended."

Red flag: threats of arrest or fines. Government agencies don't call demanding payment.

💻

The computer pop-up

"Your computer has a virus! Call Microsoft support now."

Red flag: a loud warning with a phone number. Close the window and don't call.

🎁

The surprise prize

"Congratulations, you won! Just pay a small fee to claim it."

Red flag: you can't win a contest you never entered, and real prizes don't cost money.

The golden rule

Never send money, gift cards, or codes to someone who contacted you first. Hang up and call the person or company back using a number you already trust.

Second Look gives its best guess and can be wrong. If you're unsure, ask someone you trust before doing anything.
